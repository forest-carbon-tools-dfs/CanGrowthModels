National stand-volume models of Ung et al. (2009), Nat1 and Nat2

Model ID: NationalModelUng2009

Model information

Model details
  • Developers: C.-H. Ung; P.Y. Bernier; X.J. Guo; M.-C. Lambert
  • Contributors: Not recorded
  • Organization: Canadian Forest Service, NRCan
  • Model type: whole_stand
  • Programming language: R
  • Availability: public download
  • Interface: r package
  • Output types: merchantable volume;basal area
  • Species: 25 Canadian tree species
  • Minimum inputs: stand age; degree-days above 0 C (dd0); mean annual precipitation (map, mm); species
  • Outputs: Not recorded
  • Silvicultural interventions: Not recorded
  • Other features: Not recorded
  • Management context: unknown
  • Notes: R implementation of the two national stand-volume models of Ung et al. (2009). Predicts gross merchantable volume (m3/ha) from age and climate; Nat1 also predicts dominant height and basal area. The paper intended the models for short (<10 year) projections. Parameters are published to only 4 decimals. Climate is descriptive, not for future scenarios. Nat1 has an optional bias correction that is not part of the published model. The package is experimental and MIT-licensed.
