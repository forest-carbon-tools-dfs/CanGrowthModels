This folder's ecozones.gpkg and province_ecozones.csv are derived from the
GeoPackage in source/ (the AAFC ecozones clipped to the Canada land boundary,
with the largely unforested Arctic Cordillera, Northern Arctic and Southern
Arctic ecozones removed; otherwise unchanged) by scripts/prepare_ecozones.R:
ecozone names corrected, features dissolved to one per ecozone, and geometry
simplified. See that script for the exact processing steps.
